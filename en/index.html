01 / Historical Blind Spot

We are still feeling our way through a historical blind spot

If we only treat AI as a productivity tool, we may be trapped in one of the most wasteful and regrettable cognitive limits of this transition. Using industrial-age thinking to command an intelligence-age species, reducing AI to a “superpowered tool-worker,” may mean we are standing inside a historical blind spot. Its real potential may be to rebuild the patterns by which civilization understands, creates, remembers, and expresses.

So my interest is not only whether AI can do more work. It is how it may rewrite the production mode of knowledge work, the relationship between humans and intelligence, and the longer boundary of human capability.

03 / External Brain

The Always-present External Brain

A personal Agent should not be only an assistant, an employee, or another subject opposed to you. It should be an extension of your cognitive and execution organs: always present, sharing context with you, thinking with you, and appearing or acting at the right moment.

Presence

Presence

Not turn-by-turn chat, and not always waiting for a task trigger. It is realtime and always there. Responding and staying silent are both choices the Agent can make.

Context

Shared Context

Reduce the information transport from human to Agent. Beyond what a person chooses to say after thinking, raw data should be acquired automatically by the Agent within authorization and boundaries.

Perception

Perceptual Extension

Device logs, software states, smart-hardware environment signals, and wearable physiological data are often ignored by humans, but they can become perceptual inputs for the Agent.

Cognition

Collaborative Cognition

Humans understand through awakened life experience; AI understands through structure, clues, and context. A good personal Agent does not merely explain the world. It helps a person see what they are going through.

04 / Long Horizon

Four Horizons

01 Long Horizon

Cross-domain Concept Search Engine and Translator

Search not for words, and not only semantic similarity, but structural relations between ideas.

One of the largest wastes in human knowledge is that different fields repeatedly discover structurally similar things, but are separated by language, symbols, and intuition. The system I imagine is not a terminology translator. It translates ways of thinking: it can restate the reasoning chain of one field in a cognitive frame another field can digest, while preserving the logical structure.

Economists speak of equilibrium, physicists of least action, ecologists of steady state, and game theorists of Nash equilibrium. These concepts are not always the same, but their mathematical structures, constraints, and intuitions often rhyme.

General education was, in essence, training people to migrate intuition across fields. But human cognition can only do this locally. With Agents, we can search for isomorphisms, near-isomorphisms, and transferable structures at a much larger knowledge scale.

What I want is not a glossary, but structural search and thought-style translation. When a biologist reads a quantum mechanics paper, the system should be able to restate the physics reasoning chain in a biological frame without breaking rigor.

Example If you enter “spiral,” it should not only return DNA, galaxies, and staircases. It should explain what spiral structure does in fluids, plant growth, social movement propagation, and musical progression.

02 Long Horizon

A Creation Space Without Scarcity

Let ordinary people turn vague ideas into works, products, organizations, experiments, or life systems.

Humanity wastes not only labor, but countless possibilities that never become real. I do not want a tool that merely generates an image or writes a snippet of code. I want a true creation amplifier: something that helps an idea cross expression, design, execution, feedback, and iteration until it becomes something others can experience.

Creation is still too hard. A person with an idea is often blocked by not knowing how to code, design, model, recruit a team, find resources, execute, or persist long enough.

The space I imagine is not a one-shot generator. It is a continuous environment from vague thought to finished artifact: novels, games, courses, communities, companies, research hypotheses, urban plans, or personal life systems.

The point is not to replace the creator. It is to stop technical barriers, execution inertia, and resource scarcity from killing ideas before they can be expressed. It helps people think and do while preserving human agency.

If the creation threshold drops dramatically, buried ideas across civilization may be released. The most wasted human resource may not be labor, but unrealized possibility.

Boundary It helps people think and do; it lowers expression and execution barriers without taking away the creator’s agency.

03 Long Horizon

Historical Microscope and Civilization Understanding Machine

Organize facts, mechanisms, debates, evidence boundaries, and verification paths into an interrogable understanding network.

Humans struggle to learn from history not only because we fail to try, but because historical records are heavily compressed. Processes, boundaries, hidden assumptions, and local experience collapse into short narratives. I hope Agents can organize facts, mechanisms, school-of-thought disagreements, evidence strength, and next verification steps so people can question the process itself rather than only learn from compressed conclusions.

Many human failures come from mistaking surface for cause, slogans for understanding, local experience for universal law, and short-term success for long-term truth.

I do not want an answer machine. I want a living understanding network that organizes facts, debates, experiments, failed lessons, hidden premises, and evidence boundaries so questions can be asked, checked, and simulated further.

If you ask why some countries fall into the middle-income trap, why a relationship slowly becomes draining, or why a talented company cannot build good products, it should not only give conclusions. It should show mechanisms, disagreements, boundaries, evidence strength, and the next thing to verify.

Further out, Agents could help with world reconstruction: not only explaining historical summaries, but letting people enter the situation, sense constraints, processes, consequences, and human positions.

Example Why do some countries fall into the middle-income trap? Why do some talent-rich organizations fail to build good products? Why does a method work in experiments but degrade in reality? Further out, world reconstruction could help people feel the process, limits, and consequences of a historical moment.

04 Long Horizon

Externalized Space for Consciousness Expression

Create a medium more dimensional than language, while still preserving boundaries.

Much pain comes from not being truly understood. Language, writing, music, and images are powerful, but still insufficient for many fuzzy feelings, value conflicts, memory atmospheres, and inner states. The system I imagine is not mind reading. It is a safe externalization space where inner states can be presented, adjusted, shared, and understood again.

Many human experiences are hard to express: vague fear, love that cannot be explained, layered contradiction, unformed inspiration, or the atmosphere of a memory that can only be felt.

The system I imagine does not ask people to simply “say the feeling clearly.” It turns a state of consciousness into a perceptible, interactive structure without flattening it.

This space might render shape, force fields, color relations, time flow, narrative branches, and value-conflict maps. Another person could enter with permission, not to spy, but to understand more closely how it feels.

This is not mind reading. It is a higher-dimensional empathy medium. If people can safely externalize inner worlds with more nuance, education, therapy, art, intimacy, and trust may all change.

Form It might present an inner state as shapes, force fields, color relations, time flow, narrative branches, and value-conflict maps. Another person could enter with authorization, not to invade privacy, but to understand more closely how it feels.
